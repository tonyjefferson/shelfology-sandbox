  • GA4 / Bing AI figures and the "Report-Cycle Signal" / top KPI-card GSC numbers are from the most recent completed reporting cycle (2026-08_Monthly_SEO_AEO_Internal_Report, generated 2026-08-10).
  • GSC snapshot figures ("Search Console Snapshot" section, top queries, and the page-level "Snapshot: 30d vs Prior 30d" column) were pulled from Google Search Console via the Windsor.ai connector on 2026-08-13 at 12:26 UTC, using a rolling window as of that moment. They will not match the report-cycle GSC figures above exactly, since those use a different, fixed calendar window dated to the 2026-08-10 GSC export.
  • Shopify figures were pulled via ShopifyQL on 2026-08-13 at 12:26 UTC and reflect site-wide (all-channel) revenue, not organic-only. The "SEO-Attributed Revenue" figures use Shopify's own referrer-source classification ("search"), not a GA4 channel grouping.
  • Page-level before/after data is sourced from SEO_AEO_Change_Log cross-referenced with the current cycle's GSC page-level export (Report-Cycle column) and the Aug 13, 2026 GSC snapshot comparing the last 30 days to the prior 30 days (Snapshot column).
  • GSC Pages-based site totals (10,296 clicks) and Queries-based totals (4,268 clicks) don't fully reconcile, consistent with GSC UI export truncation on long-tail rows; Pages-based totals are used here as the closer site-wide approximation.
